# Component: Sircilla Local Specifications

## 1. Local Context: Rajanna Sircilla, Telangana
Sircilla is known across India as the **Textile Town of Telangana** (*Netha Badi*). A generic service directory would miss the essential artisan and industrial workforce of the town. 

This component outlines the hyper-local customizations tailored for Sircilla.

---

## 2. Textile & Powerloom Trade Categories

In addition to standard trades (Electricians, Plumbers, Carpenters), the directory includes dedicated textile service categories:

| Trade Category (English) | Trade Category (Telugu) | Example Skills |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Powerloom Mechanic** | పవర్‌లూమ్ మెకానిక్ | Loom alignment, gearbox overhaul, motor fitting, bearing replacement |
| **Jacquard & Dobby Master** | జాకార్డ్ & డాబీ మాస్టర్ | Card cutting, design setup, punch card repair, dobby cylinder tuning |
| **Sizing & Warping Technician**| సైజింగ్ & వార్పింగ్ టెక్నీషియన్ | Yarn tension adjustment, creel repair, sizing roller maintenance |
| **Warp Tying Specialist** | తాన్ జోడించే నిపుణుడు (Twisting) | Auto-tying machine operation, manual warp knotting, reed fixing |
| **Textile Motor & Spares** | లూమ్ మోటార్ & స్పేర్స్ | Rewinding loom motors, pirn winder repairs, shuttle tuning |

## 3. Bilingual UI System (Telugu + English)

To ensure usability across all literacy levels:

1. **Category Cards:** Every category card displays both the Telugu and English names with a distinct visual emoji/icon:
   * 🧵 **పవర్‌లూమ్ మెకానిక్** *(Powerloom Mechanic)*
   * ⚡ **ఎలక్ట్రీషియన్** *(Electrician)*
   * 🔧 **ప్లంబర్** *(Plumber)*
   * 🪚 **కార్పెంటర్** *(Carpenter)*
   * ❄️ **ఏసీ & కూలర్ టెక్నీషియన్** *(AC & Cooler Tech)*
   * 🌾 **బోర్‌వెల్ & మోటార్ మెకానిక్** *(Borewell & Motor Tech)*
